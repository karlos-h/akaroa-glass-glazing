What we do

Glass, sorted properly.

From a cracked window that needs sorting today to a full splashback or shower glass fit-out. Residential, commercial and insurance work — based in Akaroa, and out to almost all of greater Christchurch.

  • Window & door repairs

    Broken panes, failed double glazing, misted units, jammed or draughty windows. Boarded up the same day where we can, glass in as soon as it's ready.

  • Kitchen splashbacks

    Toughened glass splashbacks, measured on site and made to fit. Any colour you like, including low-iron glass if you want the colour dead true.

  • Mirrors

    Bathroom, wardrobe, hallway, gym walls. Cut to size, edges polished, fixed up properly so they sit flat and stay put.

  • Shower glass

    Frameless and semi-frameless showers, and replacement panels for the one that finally gave up. Measured, made and fitted.

  • Commercial work

    Shopfronts, offices, cafés and rentals. Emergency board-ups, safety glass, and reglazing done around your trading hours where we can.

  • Insurance work

    Break-ins, storm damage, accidents. We've done plenty of it — we'll quote it up the way your insurer needs and deal with the paperwork side.

About

Kia ora, I'm Johnny.

Twenty-seven years on the tools, six of them in Brisbane. In 2018 my wife Gill and I brought our three boys home to Banks Peninsula — Ngāi Tahu, with ties to Ōnuku Marae just down the road. This is our tūrangawaewae (the place we stand, where we belong), so the work I do here has my name on it in front of neighbours.

Gill runs the books and answers the phone when I'm up a ladder. Between us that's the whole company — which is why you get a straight answer on price and a date we actually turn up on.
