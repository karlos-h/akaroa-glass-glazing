Akaroa Glass and Glazing — image folder
=======================================

Empty on purpose. Every image on the homepage is currently a styled
placeholder block (dashed border, sand background, filename label) so it's
obvious at a glance what still needs a real photo.

Drop the real files in here using these exact names, then follow the
"TODO: placeholder image" comments in index.html to swap each <div class="ph">
for an <img>. The alt text is already drafted in each block's data-alt
attribute — copy it straight across.

  hero-harbour.jpg       Akaroa harbour, wide landscape. Sits behind the
                         hero text at low opacity, so pick something with
                         calm areas and no important detail in the middle.
                         Suggested: 2400 x 1400 or wider.

  johnny-on-the-job.jpg  Johnny working — lifting glass, measuring up,
                         squaring a frame. Portrait crop, roughly 4:5.
                         Suggested: 1200 x 1500.

  glass-detail.jpg       Close-up of glass work in progress. Not used on
                         the homepage yet — held for the About and Gallery
                         pages.

  service-area-map.jpg   Simple map: Banks Peninsula with Akaroa marked,
                         and greater Christchurch shaded. Roughly 4:3.
                         Doesn't need to be a real map service — a clean
                         hand-drawn or traced graphic is fine, and fits
                         the brand better.

Style: real photos, natural light, unfussy. Johnny on the job and the
peninsula itself, not glossy architectural stock. Landscape photos of the
harbour and the bays are fair game if they're actually local.
